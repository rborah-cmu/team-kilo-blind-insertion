# Franka scene assets

`panda_table_shelves.xml` is the course's Lab 3 scene (`panda_torque_table_shelves.xml`)
with the Lab 3 blocks and grid markers removed; `meshes/` is its `assets/` folder.
The Panda meshes originate from MuJoCo Menagerie
(https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie, Apache-2.0).
